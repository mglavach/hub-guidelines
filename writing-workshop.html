Grade 1 Free Write

How It Works

One free write prompt each week, from the week of September 7 through the week of May 17 (33 weeks). Each month adds one skill on top of the last, so first graders grow from drawing and labeling to writing short pieces they can revise and share. Break weeks are skipped: Thanksgiving (Nov 23–27), Christmas (Dec 21 – Jan 4) and Spring Break (Mar 22–29). The last day of school is Thursday, May 20.

Keep a Free Write Folder

Every free write goes into each student's folder. In May, students choose a favorite to revise and publish, so they can see how much they have grown as writers.

Weekly Routine · 15–20 minutes

1

Say the Prompt

2 min

Read it aloud and post it on the board. Think aloud with one quick idea of your own.

2

Talk It Out

2 min

Partners tell each other what they will draw and write.

3

Write

8–12 min

Students draw first, then write. Any spelling they can stretch out is fine.

4

Share

3 min

Two or three students read their work. Name one thing they did well that matches the skill focus.
