Responsive Learning

Assessment

IXL

Daily Learning & Practice

  • Grades K–5, Reading & Math · testing window: Fall
  • Adaptive practice (SmartScore) — instruction/skill development, not formal benchmarking
MAP Growth

NWEA · Growth Measurement

  • Grades K–5, Reading & Math · Fall & Spring (2–3x/year)
  • Produces RIT score; sets growth goals, guides IXL/Reflex/FRACS pathways and conferences
ORF

Oral Reading Fluency

  • Grades 1–3, teacher-administered · Fall & Spring
  • Timed 1-minute passage — WCPM, accuracy, expression, decoding automaticity
STAR Reading

Renaissance STAR · Benchmark & Screening

  • Grades 2–4 · Fall & Spring
  • Scaled Score & grade equivalent — comprehension, vocabulary, instructional reading level
