Organized by Genre

The Hub library is organized by genre rather than Dewey Decimal numbers, making it easy for students to browse by topic and find books that match their curiosity. Nonfiction is grouped into four thematic sections, with Bible and Faith as a foundational anchor at the heart of the collection.
