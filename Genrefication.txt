Another Idea I Think Fits Westminster Even Better
Create a small sign that appears at the start of every major section:
✝️ Through a Biblical Lens
As we learn about God's world, we seek truth, wisdom, and wonder in all we discover.
That way:
•	Bible is honored as foundational. 
•	The rest of the library doesn't feel "secular." 
•	The library itself communicates your philosophy of formation, not just information. 
In Hub language, I'd almost frame it this way:
