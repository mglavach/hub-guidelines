Programs

Our reading program is built on the belief that wide, joyful reading creates the strongest readers, aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Standards for every grade level.

Wide Reading

Students read within, above, and below their level, exploring fiction, nonfiction, poetry, biography, graphic novels, and more. Genre variety builds vocabulary and empathy.

Sustained Independent Reading

Daily sustained silent reading builds stamina. From 5–10 minutes in Kindergarten to 30+ minutes in 5th grade, students grow as focused, independent readers.

AR as Motivation

Accelerated Reader celebrates reading milestones and encourages goal-setting, a tool for motivation and accountability, never a restriction on book choice.

The Bigger Picture

Why We Read

Why We Read
For Fun
Challenge
To Learn
To Relax

Reading is about so much more than earning AR points. Students read for fun, to challenge themselves, to relax, and to discover new ideas. AR is simply a tool to track and celebrate the reading that is already happening.

Every book a student picks up, whether it has an AR quiz or not, builds vocabulary, empathy, knowledge, and imagination. The goal is always a lifelong love of reading first. AR points are just one small way to celebrate that journey.

Encourage your child to read books that excite them, books that challenge them, and books that let them unwind. The points will follow.

Our writing program is built on the belief that every child is a writer, from the first labeled drawing in Kindergarten to a fully revised essay in 5th grade, aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Standards for every grade level.

Write Every Week

Regular free writes build stamina and confidence. Students get ideas onto paper without worrying about perfection, and their writing grows a little each week.

The Writing Process

Students plan, draft, revise, edit and share. Learning to reread and improve their own work matters as much as the first draft.

Writing Workshop

A short lesson, time to write, and time to share. Each month adds one new skill, so students move from stories to opinions to teaching pieces.

The Bigger Picture

Why We Write

Why We Write
To Share
To Create
To Think
To Remember

For young writers, writing is about so much more than neat handwriting and correct spelling. Children write to share their stories, to remember the moments that matter, to figure out what they think, and to create something new. A first grader's labeled drawing and a fifth grader's essay are both real writing.

We serve a creative God who uses words to speak, and every child is made in His image with a voice worth hearing. The goal is always confident writers who love putting their ideas into words. Spelling, grammar and handwriting grow along the way.

Encourage your child to write at home: grocery lists, cards for family, notes to friends, or stories about their day. Every word counts.

Our math program is built on the belief that math is more than computation; it is a language of patterns, logic, and wonder. Every student deserves both the confidence that comes from solid foundational skills and the excitement of mathematical exploration beyond grade level.

Foundation & Fluency

Building automaticity with math facts and operations through Reflex (Grades 1–5), so students can focus their thinking on problem-solving rather than computation.

Enrichment & Extension

Challenging students in Grades 3–5 with FRACS to deepen fraction and algebraic reasoning, and using IXL to stretch every learner K–5 with personalized practice paths.

Responsive Intervention

Using MAPS Math data and IXL diagnostic reports to identify students who need targeted support, responding quickly with small-group instruction and adaptive tools.
